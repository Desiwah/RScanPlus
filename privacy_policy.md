# 📜 Privacy Policy for RScan+

**Effective Date:** January 24, 2026

RScan+ is a professional utility designed to assist **employees** and **customers** with rapid price checking and product information retrieval. By turning a mobile device into a high-speed scanner, the app allows users to quickly find details available on public retail websites. RScan+ acts as a bridge to public web information; it does **not** access internal company APIs, private inventory databases, or non-public employer systems.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the RScan+ app, the following data may be accessed or stored locally on your device:

- **Camera Data** – used strictly for scanning barcodes and performing Text Recognition (OCR). Processing happens **locally** on your device; no images are uploaded to our servers.
- **User Preferences** – your selected language, country, and theme (Light/Dark mode) are saved to associate the app with your preferred interface.
- **Usage Statistics** – the app tracks which stores you select most frequently to rank them for faster access. This data remains on your device.
- **Clipboard Data** – used only when you trigger the Gemini AI feature to copy a research prompt for manual pasting.
- **Store Configuration** – the app fetches a public list of supported retail links from a remote configuration file to keep store data up to date.

We do **not** collect:
- Your name, email address, phone number, or IP address.
- Any private employer data or internal company login credentials.
- Your browsing history outside of the specific retail searches you initiate.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used exclusively to provide the app’s features, including:

- **Public Price & Info Checks** – allowing staff and customers to verify product details via public web results without needing manual assistance.
- **Supplemental Product Research** – providing a "two-tap" shortcut to Google Gemini to fetch deeper product details not present on a store's primary website.
- **Personalization** – remembering your region and interface settings for a faster, more efficient experience.
- **Local Ranking** – sorting the store list based on your personal frequency of use.

We **do not** use your data for analytics, advertising, or profiling.  
We **do not** share your data with third parties.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

All user preferences and usage statistics are stored **locally on your device** using secure internal storage.
- There are no user accounts, and your data is **never** synced to a central cloud server.
- No user data is sent to or stored on any third-party analytics services.
- Data is retained only as long as the app is installed on your device.

You have full control over your data. You may delete **all stored data** at any time by clearing the app's cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure (Third-Party Services)

We do **not** share, sell, or disclose your data to any third parties. RScan+ interacts with the following platforms only to facilitate your searches:

- **Retail Websites (WebView)** – When you view product info, you are interacting directly with public retail websites. Use of these sites is governed by their own privacy policies.
- **Google Gemini** – When using the AI research feature, you are redirected to Google’s platform. Data you provide there is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
- **Google ML Kit** – Used for on-device OCR. No image data is sent to Google servers during the text recognition process.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

As a user, you have full control over your data:

- You choose which stores to browse and which products to scan.
- You may change your language, country, and theme settings at any time.
- You are free to use or ignore the AI (Gemini) integration.
- You can stop all data access and delete all local info by simply uninstalling the app.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

RScan+ is intended for use in professional or retail environments by users who meet the minimum age requirements of the Google Play Store. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is transmitted to the developer, we do not maintain a database of user information.
