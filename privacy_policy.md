# 📜 Privacy Policy for R.Scan+

**Effective Date:** February 9, 2026

RScan+ is a professional utility designed to assist **employees** and **customers** with rapid price checking and product information retrieval. By turning a mobile device into a high-speed scanner, the app allows users to quickly find details available on public retail websites and manage personal product lists. RScan+ acts as a bridge to public web information; it does **not** access internal company APIs, private inventory databases, or non-public employer systems.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the RScan+ app, the following data may be accessed or stored:

### A. Locally Stored Data (On Your Device)
- **Product Lists** – Users can create and manage lists containing product barcodes, codes, and quantities. This data is created by you and stored strictly on your device.
- **User Preferences** – Your selected language, country, and theme (Light/Dark mode) are saved locally to maintain your preferred interface.
- **Usage Statistics** – The app tracks which stores you select most frequently to rank them for faster access. This data never leaves your device.
- **Clipboard Data** – Used only when you trigger the Gemini AI feature to copy a research prompt for manual pasting.

### B. Transmitted Data (Voluntary)
- **Feedback & Bug Reports** – If you explicitly use the "Suggest Store/Language" or "Bug Report" features, the text you enter, along with basic device information (OS version, App version), is transmitted to the developer via a secure webhook for review.
- **Camera Data** – Used strictly for scanning barcodes and performing Text Recognition (OCR). Image processing happens **locally** on your device; no video or images are uploaded to our servers.

We do **not** collect:
- Your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.
- Any private employer data or internal company login credentials.
- Your browsing history outside of the specific retail searches you initiate.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used exclusively to provide the app’s features, including:

- **List Management** – Allowing you to save and organize product codes and quantities for personal or professional use.
- **Public Price & Info Checks** – Allowing staff and customers to verify product details via public web results.
- **App Improvement** – Utilizing voluntarily submitted bug reports and suggestions to fix errors and add requested regions or stores.
- **Supplemental Product Research** – Providing a "two-tap" shortcut to Google Gemini to fetch deeper product details not present on a store's primary website.

We **do not** share, sell, or use your data for advertising or profiling.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

All user preferences, usage statistics, and **Product Lists** are stored **locally on your device** using secure internal storage (`AsyncStorage`).
- There are no user accounts; your list data is **never** synced to a central cloud server.
- Data is retained only as long as the app is installed.

You may delete **all stored data** at any time by clearing the app's cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure (Third-Party Services)

RScan+ interacts with the following platforms to facilitate your searches and feedback:

- **Retail Websites (WebView)** – Interaction is governed by the individual retailer’s privacy policy.
- **Discord (Webhooks)** – When you submit a Bug Report or Suggestion, the text content is transmitted to the developer's private Discord server for review.
- **Google Gemini** – Redirects for AI research are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
- **Google ML Kit** – On-device OCR processing; no image data is sent to Google servers.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

As a user, you have full control over your data:
- You manage all entries within your Product Lists.
- You may change your settings or delete your lists at any time.
- You can stop all local data access by simply uninstalling the app.
- You are never required to submit Bug Reports or Suggestions.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

RScan+ is a general utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is transmitted to the developer (outside of voluntary bug reports), we do not maintain a database of user information.
